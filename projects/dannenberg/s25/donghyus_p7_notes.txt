Leaves Fill the Path
Simon Shin
Program notes:
• This is a cover of a song made by Camellia, Temmie Chang, and Toby Fox. I liked the song and I thought it had some repetition so I thought patterns were a good way to build it in Nyquist.
• Patterns were used everywhere here, but the best usage of them is in treble, where the same overall melody plays over and over again, with the first three notes changing. I implemented this with nested patterns, and I'm pretty proud of it :) .
